# UIX-03 — the Goals redesign, August 2026

> A deliberate **product redesign** of the Goals experience — the gallery, the
> record, the trend chart, both phone compositions, the dark appearance and
> Today's Goal glance — extending the visual language UIX-01 established on the
> shell and UIX-02 carried into Projects and Areas. The domain rules are
> unchanged: GOAL-02 had already built a correct measurement model, and this pass
> found nothing wrong with it. What was wrong was that almost none of it reached
> the screen.

Evidence: `docs/design/assets/uix-03-2026-08/`, captured by
`e2e/uix-03-screenshots.spec.ts`. The same spec writes both halves of every
comparison (`SHOT_PREFIX=before-` and no prefix) against the same seeded
workspace (`node e2e/today-fixtures.mjs goals`), so nothing between a `before-`
and its pair differs except the product.

---

## 1. The audit: a correct domain behind a generic card

GOAL-02 shipped a genuinely good measurement kernel, and the first pass of this
work was to look for what it lacked. The answer was: very little.

| The brief asks for | Already shipped in GOAL-02 |
| --- | --- |
| measurement type, start, current, target, unit, direction | `GoalMeasurementConfig`, four strategies |
| direction of improvement | `inferGoalMeasurementDirection` — 85 → 70 decreases, and the owner is never asked to pick "ascending" |
| baseline-relative progress | `(current - baseline) / (target - baseline)` — the roadmap's decreasing form and its increasing one, in one formula |
| clamped visuals, unclamped truth | `progressPercent` clamps; `progressFraction` does not |
| target exceeded, regression, legacy Goals | all covered, with `null` rather than a plausible number wherever the data cannot support one |
| history preserved, never overwritten | `goal_measurements`, append-only in spirit; the current value IS the latest row |

**So this pass changed no domain rule, added no measurement type and needed no
migration.** The two kernel-adjacent additions are both reads: the Area's
identity on `GoalAreaContext`, and a batched `listMeasurementSeries` for
sparklines. Everything else is presentation.

The defects were all on the way out to the screen, and each is visible in the
`before-` captures:

1. **Goals looked exactly like Projects.** Both rendered through the shared
   `EntityCard`. UIX-02 had already concluded that a Project and an Area are too
   different to share a card and gave Projects `.dh-pcard`; Goals were left
   behind, so the Goals gallery went on answering *how much work is finished?*
   on the one screen whose subject is *am I getting there?*.
2. **Every Goal drew the same grey flag.** `AccentIcon entityType="goal"
   iconKey={null}` with no colour rank, on all nine cards. The card's own source
   comment said the accent "is its AREA's" — the loader simply never carried it.
   Meanwhile Today derived a tone from a hash of the Goal's id, so one Goal was
   green on Today and grey in the gallery, and neither colour meant anything.
3. **The chart never showed the target.** This is the most serious one.
   `TrendLine` scaled to the readings and drew the target only when it happened
   to fall inside them. For the brief's own acceptance Goal — 85 kg down to
   79.3 kg, target 70 kg — the target was *nine kilograms below the plotted
   range*, so the chart showed a line drifting through a five-kilogram window
   with no indication that anything remained. It answered "have I moved?" and
   silently refused "am I getting there?".
4. **The card never said where the Goal started.** Without the baseline, "38%"
   is a number to be trusted rather than arithmetic anyone can check.
5. **A chart inside a summary card inside a record.** The whole measurement
   section — hero value, pace facts, trend, history — was passed as the summary
   band's `description`. RECORD-01 removed exactly this stack of containers from
   the other records and it had been rebuilt here from the other direction.
6. **The gallery's hero was about the wrong thing.** A large tinted banner with a
   ring reading "1/8 — what you are working toward" described *alignment*
   (whether recent Task activity had touched each Goal). True, and not an
   outcome. At 390px it was the entire first screen before a single Goal.
7. **No trend anywhere but the record**, despite a rich history sitting in the
   database, and no way to ask "which Goals need me?" short of reading them all.

## 2. What shipped

### The Goal card — `GoalCard` / `.dh-gcard`

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [mark]  Reach 70 kg                 ⋯  │
│         Health & Fitness               │
│                                        │
│ 79.3 kg                    ╲╱╲___      │  ← the outcome, and its shape
│ from 85 kg  →  70 kg                   │  ← the journey, in words
│                                        │
│ ███████░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  38%           │
│ On track · 9.3 kg to go · 10 Dec 2026  │  ← state, distance, deadline
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The reading leads and the percentage is demoted to a small figure on a thin bar
— the exact inversion of the Project card, and the point of having two. The
tinted block behind the reading is the Area's accent at low strength; it is the
card's one tinted region, because a fully washed card is the "full-surface
saturated colour" the brief rules out and would leave the title fighting its own
background.

### Identity is the Area's, resolved once

`GoalAreaContext` gained `colourRank` and `iconKey`, read off the Area join every
Goal query already made — no extra query. The rank is `ROW_NUMBER()` over
creation order, the identical expression `d1-project-repository.ts` uses, so two
repositories cannot disagree about which colour an Area is. One rank paints the
mark, the wash, the bar and the sparkline.

### The chart shows the whole journey

`TrendLine` now includes the target in its vertical domain. The consequence is
deliberate: a Goal a third of the way there draws its readings across the top
third of the plot, and **the empty space below the line is the distance still to
cover**. It also gained three quiet gridlines, an optional dotted baseline
reference, text tags pinned to each reference line, and a point readout driven by
pointer or arrow keys — as ONE tab stop, because a year of weigh-ins would
otherwise put fifty focus targets between the chart and the next control.

### The record is a workspace

`RecordLayout` gained a `feature` slot: a top-level region between the header and
the summary band, for the minority of records whose primary artefact is neither a
summary nor a tab. The Goal's progress lives there, above the band, and opens
with a labelled strip:

```
START        NOW          TARGET      REMAINING
85 kg        79.3 kg      70 kg       9.3 kg to go
```

`Now` is a rung larger than its neighbours: start and target are fixed facts the
owner chose, and the current value is the one that moved. Once the target is
passed the fourth column becomes `Achieved · 113% of target`, because "0 kg to
go" is true and useless.

### Status views

All / On track / Needs attention / Completed, each a partition of statuses the
kernel evaluator already produces — no status was invented to fill a tab, and
`Completed` is the spine's explicit completion, never the derived "target
achieved". They are applied over the loaded page rather than in SQL: the
collection's order is the workspace-wide alignment ranking established before
pagination (DEBT-23) and its cursor is bound to that window, and the status is a
kernel derivation over measurements, dates and the owner's calendar that SQL has
no business reproducing.

## 3. Progress semantics (unchanged, and why they are right)

All four measurement strategies reduce to `(baseline, current, target)`, so:

```
progress = (current − baseline) / (target − baseline)
```

| Case | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Decreasing** (85 → 79.3, target 70) | `(79.3 − 85) / (70 − 85)` = 38%. Direction never enters the arithmetic — it decides wording |
| **Increasing** (0 → 7,240, target 15,000) | the same formula, 48% |
| **Target exceeded** (1,130 km of 1,000) | bar caps at 100%; the card states `113% of target` from the UNCLAMPED fraction |
| **Regression** (79.8 → 80.6) | drawn honestly; the percentage follows the current reading. Neutral language throughout — "Needs attention" is the strongest thing the product says |
| **Missing start** | falls back to the earliest reading; if there is none, no percentage is claimed |
| **Missing target** | current value and history still shown, no percentage — "told HOW it is measured but not WHAT success is" |
| **Nothing recorded** | "No measurement recorded yet", never a 0% bar |
| **No measurement at all** | "Not measured", and the definition of done takes the space the reading would have had |

The **progress bar always shows achievement**, never raw direction: a weight
Goal's bar fills left-to-right as the owner approaches 70 kg. The **chart** shows
raw direction, which is where a falling line belongs.

## 4. Data

No schema change. `goal_measurements` and `goal_milestones` are untouched, and
history is never rewritten — correcting a reading is an ordinary edit and
removing one recalculates derivations rather than mutating neighbours.

Two reads were added:

- **Area identity** on the three Goal reads, off the existing Area join.
- **`listMeasurementSeries(ids, { perGoalLimit })`** — the sparkline series for a
  page of Goals. One statement per chunk of ids; the per-Goal cap is applied
  inside a `ROW_NUMBER()` partition rather than by discarding rows after
  transferring them, so a workspace with a year of daily weigh-ins on ten Goals
  moves a few hundred rows and not a few thousand.

**Changing a target** preserves every measurement; only the derived percentage
moves, because progress is computed on read and never stored. **Changing a unit**
relabels and converts nothing — GOAL-02 documented this and it remains true;
conversion needs a rate, a source of truth for it and a policy for historical
values, none of which a personal goal tracker earns.

## 5. Accessibility

- Every bar is the shared `ProgressTrack` (`role="progressbar"` with
  `aria-valuenow`/`min`/`max` and an `aria-valuetext` that is the same sentence
  printed beside it), so a card and its record announce one Goal identically.
- `TrendLine` is `role="img"` with a required summary, rendered visibly as well —
  and the summary now formats its dates the way the rest of the page does rather
  than printing raw ISO.
- `Sparkline` is `aria-hidden`: every fact it carries is text on the same card,
  and a fourth reading of them would be noise.
- Reference lines are distinguished by **dash pattern** and a text tag, never by
  hue. State is always a word; tone only agrees with it.
- The chart is one tab stop with arrow-key stepping and a live readout.
- `forced-colors` rules cover the card, the bar, the sparkline and all three
  chart reference types.
- axe scans pass on the gallery and the record at 390px (`goals-outcomes.spec.ts`).

---

## 6. What was deliberately NOT done

- **No sparkline on Today.** Today's Goal row already carries a bar; a second
  drawing of the same Goal would break the one-visual rule on the surface that
  must stay a glance. Today gained the Area accent instead.
- **No milestone redesign beyond the existing stage list.** It works, it is
  honest, and it is not where the brief's weight was.
- **No trajectory forecasting** beyond the pace figures GOAL-02 already derives
  and refuses to state when the data is too thin.
- **No new chart dependency.** Everything is hand-rolled SVG over design tokens,
  as `ProgressRing` and `TrendBars` already were.
- **No re-ordering of the gallery.** The workspace-wide alignment rank (DEBT-23)
  still orders it; the status views are the lens. See PRODUCT_DEBT.
